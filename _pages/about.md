¡Hola! 👋 I'm a **senior research scientist** at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/). My main area of research is responsible AI where I develop technologies to cultivate a healthier data ecosystem (such as [SynthID](https://deepmind.google/science/synthid/)) and to improve GenAI models. I am also interested in the science of deep learning: understanding why and how neural networks learn the way they do.

I obtained my PhD at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/)🇨🇭 under the supervision of Pascal Frossard. My PhD research centered on the [science of deep learning](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/record/306613) and investigated several phenomena related to the generalization and robustness of deep neural networks. During my PhD, I interned at Google Research in Zürich and visited Philip Torr's lab at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/)🇬🇧 . Prior to that, I obtained my MSc. from [TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/) 🇳🇱 and my BSc. from [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid](https://www.upm.es/) 🇪🇸.

If you're not sure how to pronounce my name you can watch this [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6dN7IJXsh6Q). Here's a quick tip: the double "ll" in Spanish sounds like a "y" and the "u" in "Gui" is silent.
